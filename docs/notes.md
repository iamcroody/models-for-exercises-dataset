# The project idea

A trainer that, given a muscle you want to work and whatever household object you actually have, names a real exercise from the catalog, states its gym equivalent, tells you how to substitute your object, and recites that exercise's real instructions — instead of inventing biomechanics.

## Final input

I want to train my delts but I have no gym equipment.
All I have is two filled water bottles.
Recommend one safe exercise and explain how to do it.

## Final output — normal case

Exercise: dumbbell full can lateral raise
Gym equivalent: dumbbell
Adaptation: Use your two filled water bottles in place of the dumbbells.
Fill both to the same level so the load stays even on each side.
Steps:
1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, holding a dumbbell in each hand
   with your palms facing your body.
2. Keep your back straight and engage your core.
3. Raise your arms out to the sides, keeping a slight bend in your elbows,
   until they are parallel to the ground.
4. Pause for a moment at the top, then slowly lower your arms back down.
5. Repeat for the desired number of repetitions.
Safety: Keep the movement controlled and stop if you feel pain.
